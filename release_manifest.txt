To ensure scientific comparability and commons-grade reproducibility:

Ryzen 5 7500F

32GB RAM

Windows 11 (WSL optional)

CPU-only run (GPU unused)


Data:

Dataset slice:

micro + small expanded only

(No stress-test data used in training; reserve for evaluation)



Training Intention:

“Trench model” behavior:

rationale → final

🚧 Limitations / Bounded-Hobby Envelope

Toy-scale training only

CPU-first, minimal heat

Auto-halt on NaN / loss blowouts

Not suitable for large-model training

Intended for education, research, and method demonstration

Not intended for production cognitive systems


This POC demonstrates the mechanics of Symbound Upbringing, not advanced cognition, AI life, sentience, or autonomous agency.
